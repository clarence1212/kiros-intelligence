Industry briefs · October 2026

The agentic shift, sector by sector

Read each brief alongside the Agent Value Ledger and AI Delegation Map: the briefs describe what is changing; the frameworks show what to do about it.

Financial Services in the Age of AgencyBanks, insurers and wealth managers are now letting AI agents carry out work, not just advise on it, so the advantage will go to firms that redesign delegation, accountability and controls before regulators set the rules for them.→Healthcare & Life Sciences in the Age of AgencyHealth organisations that redesign accountability before they deploy AI agents at scale will get the productivity gains; those that skip that step will take on clinical and regulatory risk they cannot see.→Education & Families in the Age of AgencyAI that acts can widen access to good teaching and care, but only if institutions decide where it may act, for whom, and under whose supervision.→Energy & Sustainability in the Age of AgencyAI is now both the biggest new source of electricity demand and one of the best tools for managing it. Firms that treat AI agents as operational infrastructure, with matching controls, will run grids, buildings and portfolios at lower cost and risk.→Supply Chain, Consumer and Manufacturing in the Age of AgencyAgents now plan, buy, sell and move goods, so the advantage will go to firms that redesign how they delegate decisions, not to those that only add tools.→
