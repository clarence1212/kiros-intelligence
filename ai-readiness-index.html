Kiros AI Readiness & Value Index · K-ARVI

How prepared is your organisation to create measurable value from AI?

A free, preliminary self-assessment: 24 statements across six weighted dimensions, linked to the four Agent Value Ledger categories. About six minutes.

How it works

Dimensions and weights
Strategy and Leadership15%
Data and Infrastructure20%
Processes and Automation20%
People and Change15%
Governance and Risk20%
Financial Measurement10%

Provisional bands

  • 0–39 Foundational
  • 40–59 Emerging
  • 60–79 Developing
  • 80–100 Advanced

Bands are preliminary and are not scientifically validated benchmarks. The composite is a weighted average of the six dimension scores, each from 0 to 100.

Linked to value

Results include readiness to pursue each value pathway: revenue, operating costs, capital and risk. Readiness is kept separate from financial value: it identifies where value could be pursued, and does not estimate returns.

Your data

Nothing is stored or sent. Your answers stay in this browser window and are cleared when you leave the page. No financial or confidential information is requested.
