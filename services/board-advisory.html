Executive AI Strategy & Board Advisory

Set direction for AI with the board, decide where it should create value, and what the board must oversee.

Why it matters

For Boards, chairs, CEOs and investment committees.

Most organisations have AI pilots. Few have a strategy that says which lines in the accounts AI should move, which decisions to delegate, where to invest and what to stop.

Boards are expected to oversee AI they were never trained to judge, while competitors and regulators move faster each quarter.

What we do

How we help

Board and executive briefings

A shared, practical view of AI and agents, their risks and the board's obligations, built around your own situation.

AI strategy and investment priorities

Where AI creates value in the business, what to build, buy or partner on, and what to stop funding.

Delegation strategy

Which decisions move to AI, in what order and within what limits, using the AI Delegation Map.

Market and regulatory outlook

How competitors, platforms and regulators are moving, and what that means for you.
