AI Governance & Assurance

Govern the decisions you delegate to AI, and be ready for regulators and independent assurance.

Why it matters

For Boards, risk and compliance leaders, and regulated organisations.

Regulators increasingly expect an inventory of AI, named owners, human oversight and evidence that controls work. Most organisations cannot say how many AI systems they run, let alone what each may do.

Our method borrows from financial audit, so evidence is organised around the assertions an assurer will test.

What we do

How we help

AI inventory and agent register

Every AI system and agent in use, including those inside vendor software, with a named owner and a risk rating.

Delegation maps and controls

What each material agent may do alone, may only recommend, and must leave to people.

Gap assessment

Governance compared with relevant guidance and standards, such as Singapore's model frameworks, MAS guidelines for financial institutions and ISO/IEC 42001.

Assurance readiness

An evidence file, a readiness rating for each control area and a remediation plan.

Disclosure

We help organisations prepare for assurance. We do not provide independent assurance to organisations we advise or invest in, and we do not offer certification.
