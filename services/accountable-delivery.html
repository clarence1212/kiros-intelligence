Delivery service

Accountable Agent Delivery

When you want one team to take an agentic system from business case to live operation, with governance built in from the start.

Why it matters

For Organisations that want delivery as well as advice, including family offices and mid-sized enterprises.

Many organisations want more than a recommendation: they want a working system, delivered responsibly. The risk is that delivery teams optimise for automation and leave accountability to be added later, if at all.

Accountable Agent Delivery puts the Agent Value Ledger and the AI Delegation Map at the centre of delivery. Boundaries are agreed before anything is built, and every decision the system makes leaves a trail people can read.

What we do

How we help

Phase 1 · Financial blueprint

An Agent Value Ledger case for one high-impact workflow, with its baseline, expected effect and full cost.

Phase 2 · Architecture

A design for specialised, resource-efficient agents, built with delivery partners and sized to the workflow rather than to the largest model available.

Phase 3 · The Accountability Guard

Runtime controls that stop actions outside the agreed mandate, a review window for human intervention, and an audit trail of decisions. The technology is provided by HyperMindQ, a company in which Kiros has an interest.

Phase 4 · Integration and people

Connection to existing systems, and a plan that moves staff from doing the work to supervising and auditing it, measured against the original baseline.

Disclosure

When Kiros leads delivery, it is not independent of the solution, and the Accountability Guard technology comes from HyperMindQ, a company in which Kiros has an interest. We disclose both in writing, keep this service separate from our independent advisory work, and do not provide assurance on systems we have delivered.
