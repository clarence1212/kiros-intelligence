AI Readiness & Value Diagnostics

Find out how ready the organisation is to create value from AI, and where the value is.

Why it matters

For Leadership teams starting or resetting their AI agenda; investors assessing a business.

Readiness questionnaires on their own tell you little about value. Financial models on their own ignore whether the organisation can deliver.

Our diagnostics do both: they score readiness across six dimensions and link it to the four value pathways in the accounts.

What we do

How we help

Preliminary AI readiness assessment

A free, self-reported online assessment that gives an indicative readiness profile in about six minutes.

Enterprise AI Value Creation Assessment

Our four-week flagship diagnostic, built on interviews, evidence review, workflow and financial analysis.

Investor diagnostics

A view of a target or portfolio company's AI readiness and value potential, for investment and value-creation plans.

Focused reviews

A shorter review of one function, process or portfolio of AI initiatives.

Flagship offer

Kiros Enterprise AI Value Creation Assessment

A four-week assessment, subject to scope, of where AI can create measurable value in your business, how ready you are to capture it, and how to govern it.
