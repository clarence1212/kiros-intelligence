Agentic Operating Model Design

Redesign roles, workflows and decision rights for an organisation where agents do part of the work.

Why it matters

For COOs, CHROs and business-unit leaders redesigning how work gets done.

When agents take on tasks, roles narrow, expand or disappear, and decision rights shift. Done badly, organisations cut the entry-level roles where future managers learn, or lose track of who is accountable.

We design operating models in which agents and people each do what they do best, with clear accountability.

What we do

How we help

Target operating model

Where agents act, where people decide and how the two work together.

Roles and workforce plan

Which roles change, the new roles that supervise agents, and the talent pipeline you still need.

Decision rights

Delegation maps for each material workflow, with thresholds and escalation.

Change and adoption

How managers and teams adopt new ways of working, and how adoption is measured.
