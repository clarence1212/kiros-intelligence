Kiros Enterprise AI Value Creation Assessment

A four-week assessment, subject to scope, of where AI can create measurable value in your business, how ready you are to capture it, and how to govern it.

Four weeks

What happens, week by week

Timing is indicative and depends on scope and access to people and information.

  1. Week 1

    Enterprise readiness

    Structured interviews with leaders and teams, evidence review and a scored readiness profile across the six K-ARVI dimensions.

  2. Week 2

    Financial opportunities

    Analysis of the accounts and operating data, and a first set of Agent Value Ledger cases across revenue, cost, capital and risk.

  3. Week 3

    Strategy and governance

    Strategic priorities, workflow assessment and delegation maps for the material opportunities, with governance gaps identified.

  4. Week 4

    Transformation roadmap

    A prioritised, sequenced roadmap and an executive report, presented to the leadership team or board.

What you receive
  • Executive report
  • Readiness profile with evidence
  • Prioritised Agent Value Ledger cases
  • Delegation maps and governance gaps
  • Sequenced transformation roadmap
  • Board or leadership presentation
What we need from you
  • A senior sponsor and a working contact
  • Access to recent financial statements and management accounts
  • Interviews with 8 to 15 leaders and team members
  • Process and system documentation where it exists

Fees depend on scope and are agreed after an initial conversation.
