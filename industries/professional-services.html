The issue

Professional firms sell expertise by the hour or by the matter. Agents that research, draft and review in minutes put pressure on both: clients expect lower fees for routine work, while firms that adopt well can deliver fixed-fee work profitably and free senior time for judgement.

The leverage model is also exposed. If agents do the work junior staff used to learn on, firms need a new way to develop the partners and directors of the future.

Common industry challenges
  • Client pressure on fees for routine and repeatable work
  • Write-offs and long lock-up on work in progress and debtors
  • Junior roles where professionals traditionally learn their craft
  • Confidentiality and professional obligations when using AI
Typical readiness barriers
  • Knowledge spread across documents with inconsistent structure
  • Partnership decision-making that slows investment
  • Pricing models tied to hours
  • Uncertainty about professional rules on AI use
