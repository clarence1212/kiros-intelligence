The issue

Family offices and the firms that serve them are adopting automation for reporting, KYC vetting, portfolio monitoring and cross-border administration. The shift from tools that answer questions to agents that act on behalf of a family or a trust changes the risk: responsibility for what those systems do stays with the office, its directors and its trustees.

In Singapore, the revised framework for single family offices takes effect on 15 June 2026, and the fund tax incentive schemes set conditions on spending and capital deployment. AI decisions in a family office should be made with these in view, and with the discretion families expect.

Singapore regulatory context

Frameworks a family office already works within

AI decisions in a Singapore family office should be made with these in view. This is general context, not legal or tax advice.

  • Revised Single Family Office framework ↗Effective 15 June 2026; existing offices have until 15 June 2027
    Monetary Authority of Singapore

    Qualifying single family offices receive a class exemption from licensing. They notify MAS, keep an account with a MAS-licensed bank, and file a short annual return.

  • Fund tax incentive schemes for family offices (Sections 13O, 13OA and 13U) ↗Page last updated 22 September 2026
    Monetary Authority of Singapore

    Conditions include minimum designated investments, qualifying investment professionals, tiered local business spending, and a capital deployment requirement: the lower of 10% of designated investments or S$10 million in qualifying investments, which include non-listed Singapore operating companies.

Summaries of public MAS material, checked October 2026. Families should take their own legal and tax advice.

Hypothetical scenario · Family offices & private wealth

Agents acting on behalf of a trust

Situation. A single family office wants an agent to prepare and route payment instructions from trust accounts, saving hours each week. Nobody has written down what the agent may do without approval.

Our approach. A written AI mandate approved by the family, a delegation map with value limits and dual approval above them, trust-deed parameters encoded as rules, and an audit trail reviewed monthly.

What good looks like. The office saves time on routine work, and every instruction remains within the trust's terms with a named person accountable.

Scenarios and worked ledgers are hypothetical illustrations. They do not describe specific clients.
