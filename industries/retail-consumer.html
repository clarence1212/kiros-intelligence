Retailers, consumer brands and marketplaces

Retail & consumer businesses

Customers' agents are starting to do the buying. Brands that agents can't read will lose share.

The issue

Open protocols now let AI agents compare products and complete purchases on a shopper's behalf. Brands that depend on advertising and shelf position lose leverage unless their product data, pricing and fulfilment are clear enough for agents to compare and trust.

Inside the business, agents can improve forecasting, replenishment, pricing and service. The prize shows up in gross margin and inventory, so it should be measured there.

Common industry challenges
  • Margin pressure from promotions and markdowns
  • Inventory and write-offs from forecasting errors
  • Rising cost to serve online customers
  • Visibility in agent-led buying
Typical readiness barriers
  • Product data not structured for agents to read
  • Forecasting spread across spreadsheets and teams
  • Pricing and promotion decisions without clear limits
  • Customer data across separate channels

Hypothetical scenario · Retail & consumer businesses

When customers' agents do the buying

Situation. A consumer goods maker's retail partners have joined agentic commerce, while its own planning agents run without spending limits.

Our approach. An agent-readiness review of product data and pricing, delegation policies for the planning agents, and a deliberate plan for the roles that change.

What good looks like. The company stays visible and trusted in agent-led buying, and its own agents work within clear limits.
