Planning, physical AI and agentic commerce

Supply chain, consumer & manufacturing

Agents now plan, buy, sell and move goods. Brands that agents can't read will lose share.

The issue

Agents are moving from pilots into planning, procurement and logistics, and humanoid robots are entering factories. On the selling side, open protocols now let AI agents compare products and complete purchases on a shopper's behalf.

Value is shifting towards whoever controls the agent. Brands that depend on advertising and shelf position lose leverage unless their product data, pricing and fulfilment are clear enough for agents to compare and trust.

The financial lens

Illustrative scenario · Supply chain, consumer & manufacturing

When customers' agents do the buying

Situation. A consumer goods maker's retail partners have joined agentic commerce, while its own planning agents run without spending limits.

Our approach. An agent-readiness review of product data and pricing, delegation policies for the planning agents, and a deliberate plan for the roles that change.

What good looks like. The company stays visible and trusted in agent-led buying, and its own agents work within clear limits.
