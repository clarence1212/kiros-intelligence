KIG Advisory

AI Governance & Assurance Readiness

Govern the decisions you delegate to machines, and get ready for independent assurance.

Why it matters

Regulators now expect an inventory of AI, named owners, human oversight and evidence that controls work. Most organisations cannot say how many agents they run, let alone what each is allowed to do.

Independent assurance against standards such as ISO/IEC 42001 and the EU AI Act is coming. Organisations that prepare now will pass it; those that wait will scramble.

How we help

What we do

AI inventory and agent register

Every AI system and agent in use, including vendor tools, with a named owner and a materiality rating.

Delegation maps

For each material agent: what it may do alone, what it may only recommend, and what stays with people.

Gap assessment

Governance and controls compared with ISO/IEC 42001, the EU AI Act and Singapore guidance, including MAS and IMDA.

Technical testing

Testing of high-risk systems for safety, robustness and bias, using recognised open-source toolkits.

Evidence and readiness report

An evidence file an assurer will accept, a readiness rating for each control area, and a remediation plan.

Assurance-grade rigour

Our method borrows from financial audit. Evidence is organised around the assertions an assurer will test: completeness, existence, authorisation, accuracy and occurrence. Clients arrive at assurance with evidence that is ready to examine.

KIG prepares organisations for assurance. It does not provide independent assurance to any organisation it advises or invests in.
