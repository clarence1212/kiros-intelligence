How this website handles information.

What this website collects

This website does not use cookies for tracking, does not run analytics, and does not store information you enter.

The AI Readiness & Value Index and the ledger calculator

Your answers and figures are processed in your own browser only. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and they are cleared when you leave or reload the page. You can copy or print your results yourself.

The enquiry form

The form prepares an email in your own email app. Nothing is sent until you send that email. When you do, we receive the details you chose to include and use them only to respond to your enquiry and manage our relationship with you. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and your rights

We keep enquiry correspondence only as long as needed for the purpose it was sent, or as required by law. You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you by writing to consulting@kiros-intelligence.com.

Confidential information

Please do not send confidential financial or commercial information through the enquiry form. We will arrange appropriate confidentiality terms before any engagement.
