Our approach · The Agent Value Ledger

Every agent must earn its place in the accounts

Many organisations deploy agents because everyone else is. We start from your financial statements instead, and ask which lines an agent can move, by how much, and at what full cost.

01

Top line

Revenue

Conversion, pricing, retention, share of wallet and new products that agents make possible.

02

Costs

Operating cost and cost of revenue

Hours, handoffs and rework taken out of a process, net of what it costs to run and supervise the agent.

03

Capital

Working capital and capital spend

Cash released from receivables, inventory and payables, and capital that no longer needs to be spent.

04

Risk

Losses, provisions and compliance cost

Fewer losses, errors and fraud, and lower cost to meet regulatory obligations, counted only when the evidence is in.

The question every board now faces

The delegation map
  1. Agent acts
    Within set limits, no sign-off
    Reporting, screening, scheduling, first drafts
  2. Agent recommends
    A named person approves
    Credit memos, outage approvals, supplier changes
  3. People decide
    Agents inform, never act
    Lending decisions, clinical judgement, load shedding

Every organisation adopting AI agents is drawing this map, whether it means to or not. We help leaders draw it deliberately, and govern it.
