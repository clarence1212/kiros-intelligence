Separate by design

Readiness is not return

The readiness index measures whether the organisation can capture value. The ledger estimates and then measures the value itself. We keep the two apart, so a high readiness score is never presented as a forecast of financial return.

Our discipline

Rules we hold to

Capacity is not a saving

Freed hours count only when they are redeployed to revenue work or replace hiring that would otherwise happen.

Count the full cost

Model usage, integration, data work, human oversight, validation and assurance all go on the ledger.

No baseline, no agent

If a result cannot be measured before deployment, it cannot be claimed after.

Risk benefits wait for evidence

Lower losses or fewer incidents are counted only once there is enough history to show them.

Roles kept separate

Our advisory work is independent. Where we also lead delivery, or use technology from a company in which we hold an interest, we say so in writing and never assure our own work.
