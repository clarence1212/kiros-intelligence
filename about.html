About the firm

An independent, research-informed advisory group

Kiros Intelligence Group helps boards, investors and enterprise leaders turn AI into measurable results, with responsible human oversight.

Who we are

Kiros Intelligence Group is a strategic advisory group based in Asia-Pacific, working with a network of collaborators across the United States and Europe. Since 2005 we have helped organisations turn technology shifts into decisions they can act on.

We combine AI strategy and enterprise transformation, agentic workflow redesign, corporate finance and value creation, AI governance and responsible delegation, board advisory, and implementation oversight with performance measurement.

Our advisory work is independent: we do not resell platforms or licences, and we will tell clients when not to deploy AI. Where a client also wants delivery, Accountable Agent Delivery is a separate service, and any interest we hold in the technology used is disclosed in writing.

Two practices work as one. KIG Advisory leads client work. KIG Research develops the frameworks behind it.

Founder

Dr. Elias Kairos Chen

Founder and Managing Partner

Elias advises boards and leadership teams on AI strategy, agentic transformation and governance. He holds a PhD in artificial intelligence, an MBA and an MPA (Finance & Accountancy). He is an Associate member of CPA Australia and brings an auditor's discipline to measuring what AI delivers.

He originated the Age of Agency, Agentrification and Universal Basic Intelligence frameworks, writes the newsletter Framing the Future of Intelligence, and is the author of the forthcoming book The Age of Agency. He also founded SafeGuardAI.

Independence and interests

What we disclose, and why

Clients rely on our advice being independent. These commitments set out how we protect it.

Independent advice

Our advisory services are independent. We do not resell platforms or licences, and we will tell clients when not to deploy AI.

Delivery is a separate service

Accountable Agent Delivery is offered separately from our advisory work. When we lead delivery, we are not independent of the solution, and we say so before any engagement begins.

A named interest: HyperMindQ

Kiros has an interest in HyperMindQ, whose Praetor platform provides the runtime controls used in Accountable Agent Delivery. Clients can ask us to work with another provider.

No assurance of our own work

We do not provide independent assurance on systems we have delivered, or on technology from a company in which we hold an interest.

Disclosure in writing

Any interest relevant to a client engagement is disclosed in writing before the engagement starts, and again if it changes.
