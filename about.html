About the firm

An independent strategic intelligence firm

Kiros Intelligence Group helps leaders make sound decisions as AI moves from tools to agents that act on their behalf.

Who we are

Kiros Intelligence Group is a strategic intelligence firm based in Asia-Pacific, working with a network of collaborators across the United States and Europe. Since 2005 we have helped organisations turn technology shifts into decisions they can act on.

Today that shift is agentic AI: systems that no longer just answer questions but act on our behalf. We advise boards and leadership teams on where to delegate, how to redesign work, and how to govern the decisions machines now share.

Three practices work as one. KIG Advisory leads our client work. KIG Research develops the frameworks behind it. KIG Ventures founds, backs and advises AI companies, which keeps our advice grounded in what it takes to make AI work.
