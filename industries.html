Grounded in each sector's economics

The lines that matter differ by sector, and so do the readiness barriers and governance questions.

Financial services & investment managementBanking, insurance, wealth and asset management, family offices. Agents now draft credit memos, settle claims and act for customers. Accountability stays with the institution.→Family offices & private wealthSingle and multi-family offices, trust companies, private banks and their advisers. When agents act on family assets, accountability, fiduciary duty and continuity come first.→Professional & corporate servicesLegal, accounting, consulting and corporate services firms. When agents draft, research and review, the billable hour and the leverage model both change.→Property, facilities & energyReal estate owners, facilities operators, utilities and renewables. Buildings and grids are becoming software-controlled assets. Margin follows whoever controls them.→Healthcare & aged careHospitals and providers, life sciences, aged and community care. Agents are moving into the clinical record. Governance has to move with them.→Manufacturing, logistics & supply chainsManufacturers, logistics providers and supply chain operations. Agents now plan, buy and move goods, and robots are entering the factory floor.→EducationUniversities, schools, edtech and learning providers. AI tutors are going national. Assessment, retention and child safety have to keep up.→Retail & consumer businessesRetailers, consumer brands and marketplaces. Customers' agents are starting to do the buying. Brands that agents can't read will lose share.→
